About Rajnish Narula

Rajnish Narula is Founder and Managing Director of RN Consulting Partners. His career spans production, supply chain, automation and operational excellence, including nearly three decades at Pfizer Singapore.

He believes in strategy-to-shopfloor leadership: clear direction from the top, and changes that hold up in daily operations. He is a Lean Six Sigma practitioner and coach, a speaker on operational excellence and digital manufacturing, and a member of the NTU Industry Advisory Board.
